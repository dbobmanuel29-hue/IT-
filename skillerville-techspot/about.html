My background

My journey into web development

I am studying Computer Science at the University of Port Harcourt. My studies have helped me develop an understanding of computing, logical thinking and problem-solving.

I completed my Industrial Training (SIWES) at Skillerville Techspot. During the training, I practised frontend web development by writing HTML, styling webpages with CSS and adding basic interactions with JavaScript. I created this portfolio website to put those skills into practice and to present the work I can explain during my SIWES defence.

The experience gave me the opportunity to apply what I was learning by creating and testing webpages.
