Foundational Purpose

Technology Innovation & Applied Engineering

Skillerville Enterprise / Skillerville Techspot was founded with a singular conviction: genuine digital capability is built by hands-on construction, not passive theory. Based in Port Harcourt, we equip students, graduates, and professionals with tangible technical mastery.

Our curriculum spans high-demand modern disciplines: Full-Stack Web Engineering, Cyber Security & Network Defense, Financial Market Trading, and Essential Computer Productivity.

We serve as an accredited destination for the Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES), providing university and polytechnic undergraduates with authentic workplace exposure and structured logbook guidance.
