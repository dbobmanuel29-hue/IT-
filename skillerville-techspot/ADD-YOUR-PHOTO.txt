YOUR PROFILE PHOTO

The portfolio already has a dedicated photo area on the Home, About and Contact pages.

To add your own photograph:
1. Choose a clear portrait photograph of yourself.
2. Open the folder: skillerville-techspot/images/
3. Copy your photograph into that images folder.
4. Rename the photograph to: profile-photo.jpg
5. Keep the file name and extension exactly as written.
6. Refresh the website. Your photo will replace the BST placeholder automatically.

If your photo is PNG, either convert it to JPG or change the image filename in the three HTML pages to match your PNG filename.

The photograph is stored locally in the project folder. It is not uploaded to an external image service.
