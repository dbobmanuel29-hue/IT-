Academic & Technical Rigor

The Capstone Defense Standard

At Bobmanuel, certificates are not handed out for mere attendance. Every trainee must complete and orally defend a standalone, real-world project before a panel of technical instructors.

Trainees are questioned on code structure, algorithmic decisions, security configurations, and responsive CSS choices. This simulates industrial job interviews and academic SIWES defense boards, ensuring 100% confidence.
